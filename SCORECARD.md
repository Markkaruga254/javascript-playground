# Residency Scorecard

## Weekly grading
| Component | Weight | Score |
|---|---:|---:|
| DSA | 35 | /35 |
| JavaScript | 20 | /20 |
| Projects & practical | 30 | /30 |
| Revision & explanation | 15 | /15 |
| **Total** | **100** | **/100** |

## Problem mastery statuses
- Not attempted
- Attempted (unsolved)
- Solved with help
- Independent solution
- Reproduced later
- Mastered (explain, implement, analyze complexity, handle variants)

## Spaced repetition
Revisit at Day 1, Day 3, Day 7, Day 21, and Day 45. Failed or assisted problems should be scheduled for repetition. Retention counts separately from new-problem totals.

## Weekly review
Record focused hours, new problems, mastery/revisions, project progress, exam score, energy/recovery, blockers, and next week's corrective action.
