# Examination Policy

## Weekly score (100)
- DSA reasoning and implementation — 35%
- JavaScript concepts and practical — 20%
- Project/engineering practical — 30%
- Revision, documentation, explanation — 15%

## Standing
- 90–100: Honors
- 80–89: Distinction
- 70–79: Pass with remediation
- Below 70: Fail; retake and remediation

## Exam rules
Closed-book examinations: no AI, hints, notes, solution lookup, or copied code. Use a timer. State assumptions, solve, test edge cases, analyze complexity, and explain choices.


## Remediation
Failure triggers a focused postmortem and retake after study. Track the misconception, not just the score. Advancement is evidence-based, not calendar-based.

## Monthly exams
Month 1: JS foundations + unseen Easy/Medium + debugging.
Month 2: closures, async, DOM, testing + timed DSA.
Month 3: backend/API/database practical and request lifecycle explanation.
Month 4: final SWE residency defense and mock interview.

## AI policy
- Green (learning): explanations, conceptual examples, hints, execution tracing.
- Amber (coursework): attempt independently first; AI review after your attempt.
- Red (exams): no AI or outside help.
